Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd

Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd is the founding company of KML Group, established in 1966 by the late Mr. Punyadasa Perera. As a pioneer in Sri Lanka’s distribution sector, the company has built a reputation for excellence in retail and wholesale operations.

  • Authorized distributor for Ceylon Tobacco Company (CTC).
  • Serving the Kurunegala, Puttalam, Chilaw, and Kuliyapitiya regions with district-level logistics expertise.
  • Dedicated branches and delivery teams ensuring timely fulfillment.
  • Over 50 years of continuous contribution to Sri Lanka's FMCG ecosystem.

With an unwavering commitment to quality, customer service, and operational transparency, Kurunegala Merchants has grown into a regional force supporting thousands of retailers and vendors. The company continues to embrace new technologies and distribution models to meet evolving market needs.

As part of the KML Group, it plays a pivotal role in sustaining the group’s legacy of dependable, ethical, and results-driven business practices across Sri Lanka.
