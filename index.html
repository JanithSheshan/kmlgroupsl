    Legacy of 60 Years in Business Excellence

    Founded in 1966, KML Group began as a regional distributor—Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd—under the visionary leadership of the late Mr. Punyadasa Perera and Mrs. Chandra Perera.

    From humble beginnings in Kurunegala, KML expanded across the North Western Province by 2012. Today, the next generation continues this journey, driven by resilience and innovation.

    • Established in 1966 with deep family values and community roots.
    • Expanded from Kurunegala to Anuradhapura, Kandy, and Colombo.
    • Formed strategic alliances with Chevron, Phoenix, and CTC.
