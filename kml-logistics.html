KML Logistics (Pvt) Ltd

KML Logistics (Pvt) Ltd operates as the logistics and water manufacturing arm of KML Group, managing the full-cycle production and nationwide distribution of Blue Fill purified bottled water. The company is headquartered in Kurunegala and is committed to delivering clean, safe drinking water throughout Sri Lanka.

  • Manufactures Blue Fill Water under stringent hygienic conditions at a modern facility in Doluwa.
  • Certified by SLS standards for purity and safety.
  • Automated bottling and packaging lines ensuring consistency and quality.
  • Islandwide distribution with branded delivery vehicles and trained logistics staff.

KML Logistics has earned a strong reputation in the bottled water industry by maintaining high production standards, investing in reliable transportation, and delivering exceptional customer service. Our services cater to both retail and wholesale clients including corporate offices, shops, and public institutions.

Driven by the values of health, quality, and efficiency, we aim to expand our operations and provide safe, affordable drinking water solutions to every region of the country.
