Over Six Decades of Innovation and Impact

Since its founding in 1966 by Mr. Punyadasa Perera and Mrs. Chandra Perera, KML Group has transformed into one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted business conglomerates. Spanning logistics, manufacturing, distribution, and leisure, the Group’s journey is built on a foundation of integrity, customer-centric service, and operational excellence. Our dynamic portfolio includes FMCG, automotive lubricants, certified bottled water, and entertainment—empowered by strategic partnerships with Chevron, Phoenix Industries, and the Ceylon Tobacco Company. Today, KML continues to lead with purpose, uplift communities, and shape the future of Sri Lanka’s business landscape.

Our Legacy: From Kurunegala to the Nation

The journey began in Kurunegala with the establishment of Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd on 1st September 1966. Founded by the late Mr. Punyadasa Perera and Mrs. Chandra Perera, this venture laid the foundation for what would become the KML Group. Backed by personal investment and trusted family and friends as board members—including late Mr. W.M. Nandisena, Mr. W.M. Karunasena, and Mr. P. Munasinghe—the company thrived under the strategic leadership of Mrs. Chandra Perera in the 1990s. By 2012, the Group’s distribution network spanned the entire North Western Province, with its second generation now continuing the family legacy.

Mission & Vision: Shaping Sri Lanka’s Commercial Future

In 2012, KML Holdings (Pvt) Ltd was launched under the forward-thinking leadership of Mrs. Maheshi De Alwis (Attorney-at-Law). The company expanded quickly, partnering with Chevron Lanka Lubricants PLC and extending operations to Anuradhapura (2017), Kandy (2019), and Colombo (2023). In 2023, KML ventured into sustainable manufacturing with the launch of Blue Fill Water in Doluwa, Gampola. Additional milestones include becoming an authorized distributor for Phoenix Plastic Industries across multiple districts. Most recently, KML Group entered the leisure industry with Kanlark Entertainment (Pvt) Ltd, initiating a major tourism project in Kalpitiya Bay.
