60 Years of Leadership and Excellence

Established in 1966 by the visionary Mr. Punyadasa Perera and Mrs. Chandra Perera, KML Group has grown into a trusted Sri Lankan conglomerate spanning logistics, distribution, manufacturing, and leisure.

  • Legacy of Trust: 60+ years of ethical business practices and customer-first focus.
  • Nationwide Impact: Serving across provinces in FMCG, lubricants, bottled water, and entertainment.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Collaborations with Chevron, Phoenix Industries, and Ceylon Tobacco Company.

Our Legacy of 60 Years

KML Group was started as a distribution company confined to Kurunegala. Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd, the oldest branch under the umbrella of KML Group, was established on 1 September 1966 by the late Mr. Punyadasa Perera together with his wife, Mrs. Chandra Perera.

He invested his own money and invited his brothers-in-law, late Mr. W.M. Nandisena (Attorney-at-Law), late Mr. W.M. Karunasena, and his friend, late Mr. P. Munasinghe, to the board. Since the mid-90s, the KML Group operated under the full leadership and vision of Mrs. Chandra Perera. Kurunegala Merchants (Pvt) Ltd expanded its presence throughout the North Western Province by 2012. Today, it is proudly managed by the next generation of the late Mr. Punyadasa Perera.

Mission & Vision: Transforming the Corporate Landscape

In 2012, KML Holdings (Pvt) Ltd. was introduced under the visionary leadership of Mrs. Maheshi De Alwis (Attorney-at-Law). This strategic move led to significant partnerships, including Chevron Lanka Lubricants PLC, with regional expansion to Anuradhapura in 2017, Kandy in 2019, and Colombo in 2023.

KML Holdings (Pvt) Ltd also ventured into the bottled water industry through the launch of Blue Fill Water in Doluwa, Gampola, in 2023. The company further expanded as an authorized distributor for Phoenix Plastic Industries in Kurunegala, Anuradhapura, and select Colombo districts.

Recently, KML Group diversified into the leisure industry with Kanlark Entertainment (Pvt) Ltd., aiming to develop a world-class tourism center at the beautiful Bay of Kalpitiya.
